Privacy Policy – DayPlanner Pro

Last updated: July 2026

DayPlanner Pro stores your tasks, goals and preferences locally on your device. We do not operate a user account server or collect sensitive personal information.

Data stored on device

  • Tasks, goals, reminders and app settings
  • Star balance and shop purchases (local record)

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing – for in-app purchases (handled by Google)
  • Remote config – we fetch a JSON file to check IAP availability (no personal data sent)

Notifications

Task reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No reminder content is sent to external servers.
